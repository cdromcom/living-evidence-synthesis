# Design and dev plan: improve it using two independent critiques (v1, 2026-11-25)

You are a senior product designer and front-end engineer. You get (A) a one-page design and dev summary for the LIVES redesign and (B) two independent critiques of it, one from Claude Fable 5.2 and one from GPT-6.2 Sol. Produce an improved plan. Structure, at most 900 words:
1. **Adopted**: each critique point you adopt, one line each, and why.
2. **Rejected or deferred**: each point you do not adopt, and why (cost, risk, scope, or you disagree).
3. **Revised decisions**: the UI, UX and IX decisions as a short list (visual language and components; reading and checking-evidence flows and information architecture; keyboard, focus, motion, feedback). Be specific enough to build from.
4. **Revised phase plan**: five phases or fewer, each with a testable acceptance check.
5. **Three things to decide with the owner**.
Keep choices conservative and reversible. Do not repeat the summary or the critiques.
