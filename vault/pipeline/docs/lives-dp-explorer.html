How the figures were gathered

Costs come from the billed amounts in the call logs of the pilot runs (four papers, batch mode, 26 to 27 Nov 2026), the most recent call per step and paper. Quality-signal rating cost is the measured figure with two raters plus an adjudicator; the three-rater cost is an estimate. Times are wall-clock seconds from runs or timed tests; most stages were not timed before 27 Nov 2026 and now record their own time. The "could an open or cheaper model do this" lines are assessments, not tests.
