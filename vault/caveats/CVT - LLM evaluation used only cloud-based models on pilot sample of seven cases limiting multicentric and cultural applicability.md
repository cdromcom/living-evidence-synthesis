### Limitation

The study used only cloud-based LLMs and a small pilot sample of seven case scenarios. LLMs may face challenges with multicentric studies due to inability to recognize cultural, linguistic, and geographical differences. The seven cases were designed for training purposes, and the study's focus on a small number of scenarios limits the ability to generalize findings.

### Supporting Quote

> [!info] Quotes
> "Since this is a pilot study, it is limited to exploring the AI's potential with only a few case scenarios. Moreover, we prompted universal queries across all case scenarios, as the intention of the present study is to assess the ability of LLMs as screening tools to identify ethical issues... Furthermore, LLMs may face additional challenges in reviewing multicentric studies, as they may be unable to recognise cultural, linguistic and geographical differences." (Sridharan & Sivaramakrishnan, 2025, p. 132)
