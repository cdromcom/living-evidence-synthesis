### Limitation

The review quality findings depend on venue-specific guidelines (code of ethics, area chair guidelines, prior year statistics) that require yearly updates, limiting long-term stability. Additionally, human preference evaluations are subject to inherent biases (verbosity, position, self-enhancement), which may not fully reflect objective review quality.

### Supporting Quote

> [!info] Quotes
> "A problem with applying the prediction of human preferences to reviews is that different people may prefer different reviews. A Kaggle competition over a dataset of human preferences provides a common ground for prediction. Correcting for human bias helps partially mitigate this gap, as personal preferences are driven by human behavioral bias." (Tyser et al., 2024, p. 11)
