### Limitation

The prompts used to instruct GPT-4 were developed iteratively throughout data collection rather than being prespecified, and data entry processes (copy-paste of Methods/Results sections) were also determined iteratively. This lack of pre-registration and iterative prompt refinement means the reported agreement levels may reflect an optimized protocol rather than typical zero-shot performance, and independent replication may yield different results.

### Supporting Quote

> [!info] Quotes
