### Limitation

ChatGPT's token limit prevented evaluation of full research articles; only abstracts were assessed. This restricts the scope of evaluation to abbreviated representations of research, limiting conclusions about ChatGPT's ability to assess full reporting quality and methodology.

### Supporting Quote

> [!info] Quotes
> "Current limitations include the token limit, restricting its usage to abstracts. As AI technology advances, future versions like GPT4 could offer more reliable, comprehensive evaluations, enhancing the identification of high-quality research and potentially improving patient outcomes." (Roberts et al., 2023, p. 1)
