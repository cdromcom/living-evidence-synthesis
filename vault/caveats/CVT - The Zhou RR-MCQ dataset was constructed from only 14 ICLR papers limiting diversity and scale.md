### Limitation

The RR-MCQ evaluation dataset was limited to 196 questions from only 14 ICLR papers, making the test set small and potentially unrepresentative of the full spectrum of peer review tasks.

### Supporting Quote

> [!info] Quotes

Due to the high cost of designing high-quality questions, we limit the total number of questions to about 200 (196 to be specific)." (Zhou et al., 2024, p. 9341 [PDF p. 1])
