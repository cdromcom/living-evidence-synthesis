### Limitation

The annotators who provided the in-context examples used to guide the LLM also classified the test ideas. Additionally, the same system (Radensky et al.'s Scideator) that generated many training ideas also generated many test ideas. This creates potential circular dependency that may inflate measured performance relative to truly independent evaluation.

### Supporting Quote

> [!info] Quotes
> "Additionally, our definition of novelty relies on expert annotations, and the same annotators who provided the in-context examples also classified the test ideas. This could potentially give our approach an advantage in understanding our view of novelty. Moreover, many of the ideas used for testing were generated by the same system (Radensky et al.) that produced the in-context examples." (Shahid et al., 2025, p. 8)
