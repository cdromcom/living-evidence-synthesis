### Limitation

The study evaluated only three AI platforms (Poe Assistant, ChatGPT, Google Bard) using ten prevalidated case studies from a single handbook (FERCAP/SIDCER). This is described by the authors as a preliminary attempt, and the small number of platforms and case studies limits how broadly findings can be generalized to other AI tools or real-world IRB scenarios.

### Supporting Quote

> [!info] Quotes
> "This is the first study assessing the decision-making abilities of AI tools concerning the roles and responsibilities of IRB members and in drafting IRB SOPs. Although we have evaluated AI's potential by evaluating 10 case studies, a more comprehensive assessment should be conducted. Furthermore, future studies should focus on providing access to international and local regulations and evaluating the congruence of AI outputs." (Sridharan & Sivaramakrishnan, 2024, p. 89)
