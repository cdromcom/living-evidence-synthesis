### Limitation

Four of the five LLMs (Claude-2, GPT-4-32k-0613, GPT-3.5, Mixtral-8x22B) were text-only and could not see figures, flowcharts, or image-rendered tables. PRISMA flow diagrams, AMSTAR-relevant search-strategy tables, and PRECIS-2 design illustrations are routinely embedded as images, so non-multimodal LLMs were systematically blind to information humans use during the same appraisal. This handicaps performance estimates for non-multimodal models and may understate what current vision-capable LLMs can do.
