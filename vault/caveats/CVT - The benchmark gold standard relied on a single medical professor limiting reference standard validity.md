### Limitation

The gold standard against which LLM performance was measured was established by a single experienced medical professor, limiting the objectivity and generalizability of the benchmark. Although the benchmark was validated by a second epidemiologist, a single-expert reference standard may introduce domain-specific biases and reduces the reliability of the performance estimates.

### Supporting Quote

> [!info] Quotes
> "Furthermore, the study's reliance on a single highly experienced medical professor as the benchmark, while valuable, might limit the generalizability of the findings. A larger panel of experts with diverse areas of specialization might provide a more comprehensive reference standard for evaluating LLM performance." (Akyon et al., 2024, p. 22)
