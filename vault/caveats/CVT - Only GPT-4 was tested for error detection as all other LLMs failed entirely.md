### Limitation

All experiments in the main study were conducted exclusively with GPT-4 because it was the only model that showed any success in a pilot study. All other tested models (Bard, Vicuna, Koala, Alpaca, LLaMa, Dolly, OpenAssistant, StableLM) failed to detect errors in any of the 13 papers. This limits generalizability of findings about LLM performance on reviewing tasks to GPT-4 specifically.

### Supporting Quote
