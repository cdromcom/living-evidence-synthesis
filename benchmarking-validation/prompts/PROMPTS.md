## Relevancy: review criterion (shown after the EVD's review question(s))

LIVES is a living evidence synthesis on automated technologies (especially large language models) for research evaluation: peer review, evidence appraisal, error detection, citation integrity and reporting-quality assessment. An EVD (evidence node) is relevant when it reports how well an AI system performs a research-evaluation task, against humans, ground truth or other systems. It is irrelevant when it reports something else, for example human-only statistics about the reference standard, meta-research findings produced with an LLM rather than findings about the LLM's accuracy, or the generation of documents rather than the evaluation of research. It must also be worth learning: a specific, clear finding about such a system's performance.

Question: *Is this EVD relevant to the REVIEW QUESTION(S) and the REVIEW CRITERION, and worth learning? Yes means Relevant; No means Irrelevant.*

## Fidelity (version 3, used in run r1)

Question: *Is the gist of the EVD statement in the quoted text? Choose Met when its main finding is there; missing specific numbers or details do not matter.*

### Options

- **Met**: The gist of the EVD statement (its main finding) is in the quoted text. Specific numbers and details that match closely are a bonus, not a requirement. Paraphrase, rounding, derived figures, abbreviations and study-context wording that is not in the quote are fine.
- **Partially Met**: Use sparingly. Only part of the EVD statement's main finding is in the quoted text, or the statement says something noticeably different from what the quote says.
- **Unmet**: The main finding of the EVD statement is not in the quoted text, or the quote contradicts it.

Worked examples (EVD 1-3, JP's Fidelity ratings; EVD 1-3 are skipped for this task):

- EVD 1: 39.18% of 3063 annotated biomedical citation instances contained accuracy errors -> **Met**. The gist (a large share of citations had accuracy errors) is in the quote: it says 60.82% were accurate and gives the 21.16% minor and 18.02% major error shares, so 39.18% follows directly. The exact figure is a bonus.
- EVD 2: 57.4% of 308 researchers found GPT-4 feedback helpful and 82.4% found it more beneficial than at least some human reviewers -> **Met**. The gist (more than half found the feedback helpful, and 82.4% found it more beneficial than at least some human reviewers) is in the quote and the numbers match. The number of researchers (308) is context and does not need to be in the quote.
- EVD 3: AI platforms drafted SOPs covering fundamental sections with variations across platforms -> **Met**. The gist (the platforms covered the fundamental sections, with variations) is in the quote. A faithful paraphrase with no numbers to check.

## Grounding: Quote

Question: *Does the screenshot shown with the quote correctly show the quoted text?*

### Options

- **Correct**: A screenshot on the evidence page shows the quoted text word for word (the screenshot must match the quote text; trivial differences such as hyphenation, line breaks or quote marks are fine). A wrong or different page number in the citation does not matter.
- **Partially Correct**: The screenshots show only part of the quoted text, or the quote text has small unmarked edits or omissions compared with the text in the screenshots.
- **Incorrect**: The screenshots show a different passage from the quote, or the quote text is materially different from the text in the screenshots.
- **Missing**: No screenshot is shown on the evidence page.
- **NA**: There is no quote to check.

## Grounding: Table/Figure

Question: *Does the evidence page show the correct table or figure for the EVD statement's numbers?*

### Options

- **Correct**: A screenshot shows the table or figure that holds the EVD statement's numbers or main finding (the one the caption or the paraphrased text describes), complete enough to read the relevant values.
- **Partially Correct**: The right table or figure, but cropped so the relevant cells are missing, or only one of several needed.
- **Incorrect**: The screenshots shown are not that table or figure (for example body text only, or a different table or figure).
- **Missing**: The EVD statement's numbers appear to come from a table or figure (the caption or paraphrased text mentions one, or the numbers are too detailed to come from the quote alone) but none of the screenshots shown is that table or figure.
- **NA**: The EVD statement's numbers are fully given in the quoted text and no table or figure is referenced, so none is needed.

Rule (JP, 2026-11-23): if no table/figure screenshot is shown the answer is Missing and no model is called.
